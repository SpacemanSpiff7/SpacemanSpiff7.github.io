Does “in season” mean it is at my store?

It means the selected month falls within a documented season. Actual stock depends on the grower, weather, region, and store. Each calendar keeps its source and geographic scope.

How do catalog updates work?

The native app checks for verified data updates when opened or brought to the foreground, at most once a day. If a download fails or you are offline, the saved catalog remains available.

Why do some varieties have no dates or peaks?

Each variety needs its own evidence. We do not copy a parent crop’s dates into its varieties, and a peak appears only when a source explicitly identifies one.
