California Seasonal Produce

Support

California Seasonal Produce is a guide to typical California seasons. It does not report live store inventory or guarantee that a food is available today. If you need help, have a bug report, or want to suggest an improvement, please get in touch.

Email

contact@simonelongo.com

What to include

Include your device model, iOS version, app version and build, the food and calendar involved, a short description, and steps to reproduce the issue. Do not include payment details or other information that is not needed to diagnose the problem.

Reminders

Use a food's detail page or My Seasons to edit its reminder. Automatic reminders follow the selected calendar's month-based season. Personal reminders use a future date at 9 a.m. Pacific time and can repeat yearly. The editor shows the next reminder in Pacific time. A notification may arrive quietly or in Scheduled Summary according to iOS settings.

If notification permission was declined, open iOS Settings → Notifications to change access, then return to the app to reconcile your saved reminders. Turning reminders off leaves saved foods and calendar selections intact.

Widgets

Add California Seasonal Produce from the Home Screen widget gallery. A widget may retain its last valid snapshot while the app is unavailable. Open My Seasons to refresh saved choices. If a removed food remains visible, remove and add the widget again, then contact support with the build and iOS version.

Privacy

Saved foods, calendar choices, reminder settings, reminder history, and widget snapshots stay on your devices. For details about catalog downloads, local notifications, optional tips, support correspondence, and deletion, see the Privacy Policy.
