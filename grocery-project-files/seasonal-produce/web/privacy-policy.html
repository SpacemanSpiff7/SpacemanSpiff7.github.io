Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 5, 2026

This policy covers California Seasonal Produce for iPhone and iPad, provided by Simone Longo, and the related produce project and support pages on simonelongo.com.

1. Your browsing stays on your device

The native app does not require an account. Searches, browsing, and sort preferences are processed on your device. It does not request your location, include advertising, upload contacts, or use tracking or analytics SDKs.

2. Catalog downloads

A produce catalog is bundled with the app and saved for offline use. When connected, the app checks for verified catalog updates when opened or brought to the foreground, at most once a day.

Downloads are served by Cloudflare. These requests expose ordinary connection information, such as your IP address and request time, to the hosting provider for delivery and security. They do not include your searches or browsing history. We do not use these requests to build advertising profiles. See Cloudflare’s privacy policy for its handling of connection information.

3. Website and external links

The web version is separate from the native app. The main web produce page and some other project pages load Google Analytics with analytics and advertising storage denied by default. Google may receive cookieless measurement signals and ordinary connection information even when storage is denied. The native app and these privacy and support pages do not load that analytics script.

The website is served through GitHub Pages and Cloudflare, and pages may load fonts from Google. These providers receive normal web request information. Their practices are described in the GitHub privacy statement and Google privacy policy.

Source links open third-party websites with their own privacy policies. We do not control those websites’ collection practices.

4. Contacting support

If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. We use it to answer your request and resolve reported issues. Correspondence is retained while needed for those purposes or applicable obligations. You can ask us to delete information you have sent by contacting the address below.

5. Your choices

You can use the saved catalog offline. Deleting the app removes its local catalog and preferences. We do not sell personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

6. Changes to this policy

Material changes will be reflected here with an updated effective date. App Store privacy disclosures will be reviewed when the app’s data practices change.
